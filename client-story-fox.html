THE CHALLENGE

The Challenge

The owner’s previous account had been suspended, so this engagement did not begin with an inherited audience or a mature connection graph. The new account had to be rebuilt from zero inside a pure entertainment niche, with one unusually strict requirement: every connection should be valuable, relevant and capable of strengthening the distribution of the content rather than inflating the account with low-quality numbers.

STRATEGY

Our Approach

  • Built the account from zero through a fully delegated management model, treating connection quality as infrastructure rather than as a vanity metric.
  • Created a high-frequency entertainment publishing system around viral videos, highly engaging clips, trending visual moments and shareable entertainment formats—not a news-feed model.
  • Applied aggressive but content-faithful hooks, post framing and publishing timing so individual pieces had a better chance of moving beyond the immediate follower base.
  • Expanded the connection graph daily around accounts and audiences likely to value, engage with and redistribute the entertainment content.
  • Managed the account as a full operating system: content selection, hook design, publishing, connection development and reward-plan qualification were coordinated rather than handled as separate tasks.
