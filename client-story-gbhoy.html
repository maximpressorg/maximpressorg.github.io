THE CHALLENGE

The Challenge

The account entered as a mid-range profile with roughly 9K total followers and approximately 50K verified home-timeline impressions already recorded. The assignment was to build a credible path toward the 500K Organic Content Reward threshold through original publishing, not through volume for volume’s sake.

SCOPE

What We Worked On

Organic Content CreationPublishing StrategyVerified Home-Timeline GrowthDelegated Account Management
STRATEGY

Our Approach

  • Built a daily organic-content plan and handled regular publishing through delegation so the account could operate on a repeatable schedule.
  • Created original content through the MaxImpress team and paired it with post-level hook and publishing decisions tailored to the account’s audience behavior.
  • Used consistent release cadence and connection-aware distribution to increase the probability that strong posts would travel into larger verified audiences.
  • Tracked verified home-timeline progress separately from public view counts to keep the engagement aligned with the actual qualification requirement.
