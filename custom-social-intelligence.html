What We Do

  • Search publicly available information across multiple social media platforms

  • Filter findings by location, industry, audience, keywords, or other criteria
  • Organize results into a clear and usable format
  • Support lead generation, market research, outreach, and business development
02

Construction Company Example

A construction company may need information about people who are:

  • Planning to build a house
  • Looking for construction services
  • Searching for contractors
  • Discussing renovation or property development
