How to Make Money on X in 2026

The main ways creators can earn through X itself and through the audience they build around their niche.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
THE OPTIONS

There is more than one route

01

Original Content Rewards

X’s new rewards program pays eligible creators based on qualified impressions generated by original content.

02

Creator Subscriptions

Eligible creators can offer paid monthly subscriptions for exclusive content and closer access.

03

Brand partnerships

Niche authority and audience fit can create sponsorship or partnership opportunities outside X’s direct payout programs.

04

Affiliate opportunities

A creator can earn commissions by referring an audience to relevant third-party products or services, subject to applicable disclosure rules.

05

Products and services

X can act as a discovery and lead channel for a creator’s own consulting, product, course, newsletter or business.

06

Tips and external support

Where available, one-off support can supplement other revenue, but it should not be treated as predictable income.

COMMON THREAD

What tends to matter across every route

Every revenue model depends on trust. Platform rewards need qualifying content and reach. Subscriptions need loyalty. Brand deals need audience fit. Products and services need credibility and a clear offer. That is why building the audience itself is often more valuable than obsessing over a single monetization button.

EXPECTATIONS

Do not treat estimates as promises

Income varies.

X does not publish one universal fixed rate per impression. Brand rates, subscription revenue and off-platform sales also vary. Use examples to understand possibilities, not to promise what a particular account will earn.
