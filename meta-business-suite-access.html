Manage Instagram & Facebook Without Sharing Passwords

How official Meta access tools can support a safer client-management workflow for Pages and linked Instagram accounts.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
SECURITY

Use platform permissions instead of routine password sharing

Meta supports Page access and task access. People with task access can work through tools such as Meta Business Suite and Ads Manager, depending on the permissions granted. This can let a client keep ownership of the account while giving a service provider only the access needed for the agreed work.

The principle is simple

01

Client keeps ownership

The business owner should remain in control of the core account and access settings.

02

Grant only what is needed

Use the lowest practical level of access for content, messages, insights or ads.

03

Review permissions

Remove old team members or agencies when access is no longer required.

04

Separate platform access from payment access

Managing content should not require control of the client’s personal banking or unrelated accounts.

WORKFLOW

What MaxImpress uses this for

For managed Instagram/Facebook work, official access can support publishing, profile management, engagement and analytics workflows while reducing unnecessary credential sharing. The exact permissions depend on the client’s setup and Meta’s current tools.
