A Better Meta Content Strategy for Instagram & Facebook

Build a simple, repeatable content system that uses Reels, posts, Stories and community interaction without turning the account into a posting machine.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
FORMAT MIX

Use each format for a reason

R

Reels

Discovery, demonstration, personality, quick education and shareable ideas.

P

Feed posts & carousels

Clear explanations, proof, visual storytelling, offers and content people may save.

S

Stories

Frequent lightweight updates, polls, behind-the-scenes context and direct audience interaction.

C

Comments & DMs

Relationship-building, feedback and conversations that reveal what the audience actually needs.

STRUCTURE

Build around content pillars

Choose three to five repeatable pillars that connect to the brand. A service business might use: education, proof/results, process, founder perspective and offers. A creator might use: expertise, personal perspective, community conversation and collaborations.

The purpose of pillars is not to make content repetitive. It is to make the account coherent enough that a new visitor can quickly understand why they should follow.

MEASURE

Review performance without overreacting

Use Insights to compare several weeks of content rather than judging the strategy from one post. Look at which topics create meaningful watch time, saves, shares, profile activity and conversation. Then produce more of what creates the right response, not just the biggest raw view number.
