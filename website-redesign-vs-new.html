Website Redesign vs. New Website: How Do You Decide?

A redesign is useful when the foundation is sound. A new build makes more sense when the structure, technology or positioning no longer supports the business.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
REDESIGN

Redesign when the foundation is still useful

If the site structure works, the content is mostly relevant, the platform is maintainable and the main problem is presentation or conversion, a redesign can be efficient. Keep what is working and improve the parts visitors actually experience.

REBUILD

Rebuild when the foundation is the problem

A new build is more practical when navigation is fundamentally confusing, the site is difficult to maintain, mobile behavior is broken, the brand or service model has changed, or technical debt makes every small change expensive.

Use this decision checklist

01

Content

Is most of the current information still accurate and useful?

02

Structure

Can visitors find the important pages quickly?

03

Technology

Can the current platform support what you need next?

04

Mobile

Does the existing site work well across current screen sizes?

05

Performance

Can speed and stability problems be fixed without rebuilding everything?

06

Brand fit

Does the site still represent how the business wants to be perceived?
