Why X Monetization Isn’t Working for You?

A diagnostic guide for accounts that cannot apply, were declined, lost monetization access, or are approved but seeing weak earnings.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
DIAGNOSE

First identify what “not working” means

The solution depends on the failure point. A missing application option, an eligibility shortfall, an unsuccessful review, a policy suspension and a low payout are different problems. Do not change the whole strategy until you know which stage is actually failing.

Many older monetization guides mix together requirements from Creator Revenue Sharing, Creator Subscriptions and the newer Original Content Rewards program. The list below separates current Original Content Rewards issues from legacy or product-specific assumptions.

The most common blockers

01

Not enough recent qualified reach

Original Content Rewards currently requires at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days. The window keeps moving.

02

Raw views are being confused with qualified impressions

Paid, promoted, duplicate, artificial and fraudulent impressions are excluded. Reply impressions are excluded from the eligibility threshold.

03

Wrong Premium tier

Basic has useful features but the current Original Content Rewards eligibility page requires Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business.

04

Account or monetization standing problems

A paused monetization status, repeated policy violations, fraud signals or integrity problems can block or remove participation.

05

The content is not original enough

Copied, minimally changed, aggregated or re-uploaded work may not generate qualified impressions under the new program.

06

The account type or location is ineligible

Program availability is country-specific, and X currently excludes political or government organizations from Original Content Rewards.

Three issues people often misdiagnose

A

“My account is too new”

A three-month account-age rule is often repeated in older monetization guidance. X still uses an account-age requirement in its Creator Subscriptions policy, but the current Original Content Rewards eligibility page does not list a three-month account-age requirement.

B

“I need more followers”

Original Content Rewards currently requires 500 verified followers, but once that box is checked, the harder issue may be qualifying Home Timeline reach and original-content consistency.

C

“I am approved, so I should earn a fixed amount”

X does not publish a simple fixed payout per impression for Original Content Rewards. Approval and earnings are different stages.

AFTER APPROVAL

If you are approved but earnings are weak

Low earnings do not automatically mean the program is broken. Payouts depend on the program’s qualifying activity and X’s current payout model, not on a guaranteed rate. Improve the parts you can control: the originality of your content, niche fit, repeat audience interest and consistency of qualifying reach.

Avoid using calculators or screenshots as promises. They can illustrate scenarios, but they cannot predict what a particular account will earn.

A clean troubleshooting order

Check program→Check eligibility→Check account standing→Audit originality→Review qualified reach→Check payout setup
