X Premium vs. Premium+: Which Should Creators Choose?

A simple comparison of X Premium and Premium+ for creators who care about reach, monetization access, Articles and a cleaner platform experience.

MaxImpress ArticlesUpdated September 2026No guaranteed outcomes
OVERVIEW

The practical difference

X currently offers Basic, Premium and Premium+. For creator monetization, the important distinction is that Premium and Premium+ include access to apply for Original Content Rewards and Creator Subscriptions, subject to eligibility. Basic does not provide the same creator-monetization access.

COMPARE

Premium vs. Premium+

Premium

  • Blue checkmark after eligibility review
  • Reduced ads
  • Access to apply for Original Content Rewards
  • Access to apply for Creator Subscriptions
  • Larger reply prioritization than Basic
  • Media Studio and higher Grok limits

Premium+

  • Everything in Premium
  • No ads anywhere on X, subject to X’s stated exceptions
  • Largest reply prioritization
  • Higher Grok limits
  • Radar Search
  • Articles
DECISION

Which one makes sense?

Premium is the straightforward starting point for an eligible creator who needs the monetization features. Premium+ can make sense when you also value the additional product features and the strongest reply prioritization. The higher tier should not be sold as a guarantee of monetization approval, reach or income.

X changes pricing and feature packaging periodically and by market, so the current price should always be checked inside X before subscribing.
